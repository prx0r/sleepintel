# GOODNIGHT WORLD (#7) — channel thesis (exemplar, 2026-09-26)

## Promise
"The world is actually getting better — sleep on that."

## Why it works
End-of-day cortisol meets its opposite signal: progress with proof. Not
"I learned something" but "I feel better about the world before I sleep."
Rides place-law (you sleep *in* a healing world) + ritual-law (nightly
habit: watch → feel better → sleep well → return).

## Sources
- Tier 1 (curated, RSS/web now): Good News Network, BrightNewsDaily,
  Good News Instead, InspireMore, PBS 5-good-news, Good Good Good,
  Optimist Daily, Reasons to be Cheerful, Future Crunch, Uplift.
- Tier 2 (owned pipes): GDELT firehose (positive-sentiment filter),
  ReliefWeb successes, HDX progress data, World Bank Data360 indicators.
- Tier 3: none — Tier 1+2 already oversupply a daily 5-story pick.

## Pipeline (E5 talk + E6 freshness)
Daily: pull RSS + GDELT + ReliefWeb + Data360 → dedupe → rank by
heartwarming factor → pick 5 → rewrite warm (data becomes story:
"poverty down 2%" → "fewer families going hungry tonight") → edge-tts
0.7× warm → low ambient (rain/fire) → slow PD/CC slideshow → encode.
Cost: ~zero. No new APIs needed — all pipes held.

## Retention mechanic
Nightly proof the world heals → better sleep → morning credit → tonight
again. Habit, not learning.

## Rights
Safe: rewritten summaries (commentary) + data indicators (facts, not
expression). Same practice as the sources themselves.

## First 3 pilots
1. "Five proofs the world healed this week" · 2. "Vietnam, Morocco,
Rwanda: tonight's evidence" · 3. "The quietest good week on record".

## Kill criteria
No return-viewer lift by episode 14 (daily = fast data). Below-median
retention at 30 days → kill, keep the pipes (they serve other channels).
