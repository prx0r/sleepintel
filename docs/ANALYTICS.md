# ANALYTICS — future telemetry (v1, 2026-09-26)

Built later, designed now. Three views, in order of value.

## 1. RPM vs actuals

Per video: predicted band (from category + length + geography mix) vs
realized RPM (Analytics revenue). Delta tracked per channel, per engine.
Questions: which shelf over/under-earns its band? Does hosted beat ambient
on money as H-HOST claims? Resolution feeds H-HOST and H-AMBIENT directly.

## 2. Competitor graph

Nodes: high-performing channels by shelf (from research files). Edges:
shared topics, shared formats, shared audiences (comment overlap where
visible). Purpose: see HOW they do it (packaging, cadence, title grammar),
not just what. Updated monthly; snapshots immutable.

## 3. Content analytics (our own)

Feature-level: which accents, paces, hands, intros, title grammars retain?
Variant matrices (already spec'd in VIDEOS.md) resolve here. Intro quality
scored separately — first-five-seconds retention as its own metric, per
the STYLE law. Findings become engine defaults (the organism learning).
