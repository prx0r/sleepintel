# #82 SLEEPY TIMETABLES — channel thesis (filed verbatim, 2026-09-26)

Pitch: "14:47. London Paddington. Reading, Newbury, Exeter. Arrive 17:23.
Platform 4. Coach D. Quiet carriage."

## Sources

- National Rail (UK) — live schedules — free API.
- Amtrak (US) — free web. Deutsche Bahn — free API. SNCF — free API.
- Historical timetables (Victorian/Edwardian) — PD via Gutenberg.

## Content structure (per route)

1. The station: "London Paddington. Opened 1854. Brunel's masterpiece."
2. The route: "Leaves 14:47. Reading, Newbury, arrive Exeter 17:23."
3. The journey: "Thames Valley to Berkshire Downs. The long climb to Devon."
4. The rhythm: "Platform 4. Coach D. Quiet carriage. The window seat."
5. Goodnight: "And now you know. Sleep well."

## Why it works

Most sleep-inducing content possible; rhythmic; predictable; cultural
(each route its own character); infinite (every route in the world).

## Production

Timetable → extract schedule → station history → edge_tts 0.7× very slow →
ambient (train sounds optional) → visual: train, track, station.
