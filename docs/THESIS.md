# SLEEPINTEL — thesis (v1, 2026-09-26)

Sleep is a location, not an achievement. Nobody clicks "for sleep" — they
click where they want to feel like they are: a tent in rain, a 1700s
cottage, inside a nocturne, beside a dying fire with a story. Sleep is how
they consume; place is why they click.

## The five laws

1. **Place, not topic.** Every offering sells somewhere to *be*. Curiosity
   gets the click; habitation keeps the night.
2. **Engines, not channels.** A channel is an expression of an engine
   (corpus + accent + host flavor). Build six engines, get fifty channels.
   Never build an engine for one channel.
3. **Hosted beats abandoned.** One recognizable voice, one goodnight ritual,
   irregular soft check-ins. The voice is the logo; repetition is devotion;
   the host who replays with you is company, not content.
4. **Difficulty is a lullaby.** Hard papers, dense theory, complex cosmology
   — tracking-load near zero is what sleeps people, not simplicity.
   Interesting enough to choose; forgiving enough to drift from.
5. **Data grows the organism.** Every pilot is emitted with tracked IDs;
   YouTube API returns watch behavior; verdicts promote or kill. The network
   is an organism: sense (metrics) → decide (rules) → grow (pilots).

## What sleepintel is

The incubator and the nervous system. It does not render video — engines
do that. It decides what gets rendered, tracks what happened, and grows
the network: registry of engines and channels, verdict rules, YouTube
feedback loop, pilot queue. All data tracked, everything receipted.
