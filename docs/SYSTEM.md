# SYSTEM — how sleepintel works (v1, 2026-09-26)

## The loop in one paragraph

Theses become hypotheses with thresholds. Hypotheses become channels with
corpora. Channels render pilots with tracked IDs. Pilots collect metrics.
Metrics resolve hypotheses. Resolved hypotheses re-rank the queue. The
organism grows toward maximal RPM per engine-hour. Every step leaves a
receipt; every receipt is data.

## Calibration (views as the teacher)

Predictions ship with thresholds; returns calibrate them. Every resolved
hypothesis updates its parents' weights: demand multipliers that predicted
right grow, that predicted wrong shrink. Score.py is versioned — when
weights change, the commit message says which verdict caused it. The system
gets smarter only through recorded disagreements with reality.

## Competitor breakdowns (recipes to success)

Per outperforming channel: top 3 videos (IDs, views, dates) → intro
(first 30s, verbatim beats) → feel (pace, voice, visual, music) → recipe
(the reproducible pattern) → what we steal (one mechanism, named).
Template: docs/COMPETITOR_BREAKDOWN_TEMPLATE.md. Seeds filed for Halidon,
Stillwave, Sleep Cove, Bub, Oliver, Jung, HushTV, VSQ in research files.
Breakdowns feed TAGS (new tags as discovered) and STYLE (winning intros
become intro-pack options).

## Effort economics (the ROI layer)

Every channel carries effort/10 (operator minutes + cash, subjective but
capped and dated). Queue ranks by EV/effort, not EV: a manual-read doing
well at effort 1 beats a deconstruction at 8 until data says otherwise.
If 0-effort wins, lean in — the organism follows returns per effort,
maximal RPM per engine-hour being the goal.
