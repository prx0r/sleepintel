# SLEEPY WORDS — the synthesis (filed verbatim, 2026-09-26)

One word. One history. One journey through time. The word is the motif,
the history is the story, the vocabulary seeps in.

## Exemplars

Salary: fixed payment → Roman salarium (salt money, soldiers paid in salt)
→ worth-your-salt → salad (salata, salted greens) → sauce (salsus) →
salutation (salus, health; to salute = wish salt). "Every time you say
'salary', you're speaking Latin." Sleep well.

Deadline: final moment → literal Civil War prison line (cross = shot) →
battlefield to office, dirt line to calendar date. Sleep well.

Clue: evidence/hint → Greek clew, ball of thread → Theseus, labyrinth,
Ariadne → every detective story owes a princess and thread. Sleep well.

## Episode format (per word, 7 beats)

1. Today · 2. Origin · 3. History · 4. Journey · 5. Relatives ·
6. Reflection ("every time you say X…") · 7. Goodnight ("Sleep well.").

## Production

Word list → etymology (Etymonline, Wiktionary, Oxford API, Gutenberg dicts)
→ historical context (Wikipedia, books) → warm rewrite → edge_tts 0.7× →
ambient (optional) → visual: word transforming through time → encode.

## Themed series (seasons with endings)

War (~20: deadline, salary, clue, campaign, rank, file, officer) · Food
(salad, sauce, dessert, restaurant, cuisine) · Love · Death · Money ·
Home · Sea · Sky. Each finite, each ending starts the next.

## The moat (five-part combo)

Etymology + history + seeping vocabulary + narrative + completionism.
Etymonline does #1, history channels #2, vocab apps #3 — nobody combines.
