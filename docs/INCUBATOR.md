# INCUBATOR — ops (v1)

## Inbox (weekly triage)

One line per idea: name — parent engine guess — why. Older than a week:
promote to registry row or delete. Unblocked + under an hour: do, don't park.

## Brief (channel row)

Corpus verified (paths, not vibes) + accent + readiness + pitch. No row
without a corpus. Rights checked at brief time (PD / permission / discuss-only).

## Pilot (one video)

Same grammar as its engine. Emitted with tracked IDs (channel + variant).
Lands on the review surface with its verdict slot pre-opened.

## Verdict (weekly)

promote (→ campaigns + queue) | iterate (one variable named) | kill
(retained as data). Owner judges; generator never judges its own pilots.

## Laws

One engine per need, not per channel. No engine without three demanders.
No channel without a verified corpus. No pilot without a verdict slot.
Kill fast, keep the note. Secrets in env, never in repo.
